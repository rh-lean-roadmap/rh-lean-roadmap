一份黎曼猜想证明的故事版A story version of a proof of the Riemann hypothesis

中线以东East of the Line

2026 年 11 月 20 日，一份黎曼猜想的证明公布了。它没有论文，只有 175,187 个 Lean 代码文件，一句注释都没有。Lean 是一种让电脑逐步检查数学证明的语言。机器验过，说它对；人还没读完。下面把它讲成一桩海港里的案子，主角是一位探长、一个搭档，和一片找不到嫌疑人的海。On 20 November 2026 a proof of the Riemann hypothesis was released. There is no paper, only 175,187 files of Lean code without a single comment. Lean is a language in which a computer checks a mathematical proof step by step. Machines have checked it and say it is correct; no person has read it through. Here it is told as a harbour mystery, with an inspector, a partner, and a sea that holds no suspect.

机器检查：已出结论的全部通过Machine checks: every finished check passed 人读懂：作者说自己还没掌握全貌Human understanding: the author says he has not mastered it

故事里的探长大致对应数学家 Levent Alpöge，搭档对应 AI 模型 Claude。对话和场景是编的；每章末尾“现实里”那几行是事实。The inspector stands roughly for the mathematician Levent Alpöge and the partner for the AI model Claude. Dialogue and scenery are invented; the “In reality” notes at the end of each chapter are the facts.

先看结论，五句话讲完整个证明The whole proof in five sentences
  1. 黎曼猜想说，素数个数和预测之间的误差，最多只有 √x 那么大，再乘一个涨得很慢的倍数。The Riemann hypothesis says the error in counting primes is never much more than √x.
  2. 一条 1905 年的老道理让人只需证一个上限，只要远处总能找到一整段误差不超标的区间就够了。A principle from 1905 reduces this to an upper bound: it is enough that far out there are always whole stretches where the error stays under the line.
  3. 在只有有限个数的“小世界”里，早有人用“算式的根不会比次数多”证过类似的上限，诀窍是让每个点都成为重根。In finite “small worlds” a similar bound was proved long ago from the fact that a polynomial has no more roots than its degree, by making every point a repeated root.
  4. 整数没有重根。这份证明用一串约 √x 道“船闸”（theta 提升），让某些数被素数整除很多次，用整除的次数顶替重根。Integers have no repeated roots, so this proof uses a chain of about √x “locks” (theta lifts) to make certain numbers divisible by a prime many times, with that count standing in for multiplicity.
  5. 其余的工作是把账算平，越过 ⅔ 那道坎，挡住天生带来的整除，再把一百多年的数论地基全部写成机器能检查的代码。The rest is balancing the books, getting past a wall at ⅔, screening out divisibility that is there from birth, and writing a century of number theory into code a machine can check.
第 1 章Chapter 1

报案The report

素数港有一本老账，记的是从 1 数到 x 一共有几个素数。港务处另有一张预测表，画着一条光滑的曲线。老账和预测表总对不齐，差额像潮水，一会儿高，一会儿低。The Prime Harbour keeps an old ledger of how many primes there are from 1 up to x. The harbour office has a forecast too, a smooth curve. The two never quite agree, and the difference rises and falls like a tide.

1859 年，黎曼把这潮水拆开了。差额是许多道涌浪叠出来的，每道浪来自远海上一个固定的风眼。海图上每个风眼有一个东西坐标，中线的坐标是 ½。坐标决定浪有多凶。数到一百万时，坐在中线上的风眼掀起的浪大约一千高，正好是一百万开平方；坐在 0.62 的风眼，浪大约五千二。数到一万亿，前者约一百万，后者约两千七百五十万。数得越远，靠东的风眼越压过所有人。In 1859 Riemann took the tide apart. It is a sum of swells, each coming from a fixed storm eye far out at sea. Every eye has an east–west coordinate on the chart, and the middle line sits at ½. The coordinate sets how fierce the swell is. Counting to a million, an eye on the middle line raises a swell about a thousand high, the square root of a million; an eye at 0.62 raises one about 5,200 high. Counting to a trillion, the first is about a million and the second about 27.5 million. The further you count, the more an eastern eye drowns out the rest.

黎曼猜想说，风眼全在中线上。换成账本的话，差额永远只比 √x 大一个涨得很慢的倍数，数到一百万，这个倍数才十几。前人用计算机查过极多的风眼，个个都在线上。可风眼有无穷多个，一个个看是看不完的。2026 年秋天，这桩案子落到了一位探长和他的搭档手里。The Riemann hypothesis says every eye sits on the middle line. In ledger terms, the gap is never more than √x times a slowly growing factor, a factor only in the teens when you count to a million. Computers have checked vast numbers of eyes, all on the line. But there are infinitely many, and nobody can look at them one by one. In the autumn of 2026 the case landed with an inspector and his partner.

现实里 · 给懂行的读者In reality · for readers who know the field风眼是 ζ 函数的零点，东西坐标是零点的实部。每个零点给素数计数的误差贡献一个波，大小约是 x 的“实部”次方。黎曼猜想等价于误差不超过 √x 乘对数的量级。Storm eyes are zeros of the zeta function and the east–west coordinate is the real part. Each zero adds a wave to the error in counting primes, of size about x to the power of its real part. The Riemann hypothesis is equivalent to the error being at most of order √x times a logarithm.
第 2 章Chapter 2

把海图对折Folding the chart

搭档先把海图沿中线对折。海图有条老规矩，西边每有一个风眼，东边同一高度的对称位置一定也有一个。所以只查东边就够了。The partner begins by folding the chart along the middle line. An old rule of the chart says that every eye in the west has a twin at the same height in the east. So the east is all that needs checking.

案子还顺手扩大了。沿岸别的港口先给素数分类再数。比如按除以 4 的余数分，5、13、17 余 1，记一本账；3、7、11 余 3，记另一本。这些港口各有各的风眼，规矩和素数港一样，于是并案。还有些账本是副本，和原本只差几处固定的小改动，这些改动自己不会在中线以东生出风眼，查过原本，副本就不用查。任务缩成了一句话，证明中线以东一个风眼都没有。The case also grew. Other harbours along the coast sort the primes before counting. Sorting by remainder after division by 4, say, 5, 13 and 17 leave remainder 1 and go in one ledger; 3, 7 and 11 leave remainder 3 and go in another. Each harbour has its own storm eyes, under the same rule, so the cases were joined. Some ledgers are copies of others with a few fixed small changes, and those changes never raise an eye east of the line, so checking the original is enough. The task shrank to one sentence: show there is no storm eye east of the line.

现实里 · 给懂行的读者In reality · for readers who know the field对折是函数方程。别的港口是狄利克雷 L 函数，一起证明的结论叫狄利克雷 GRH；副本是非本原特征，它的 L 函数等于本原特征的 L 函数乘有限个在右半平面不为零的欧拉因子。一般数域的 ζ 函数不在这次的范围里。The fold is the functional equation. The other harbours are Dirichlet L-functions, and the joint result is Dirichlet GRH. The copies are imprimitive characters, whose L-function is a primitive one times finitely many Euler factors that do not vanish in the right half-plane. Zeta functions of general number fields are not covered.
第 3 章Chapter 3

只看上沿的警戒线A line that only watches the top

探长不打算出海抓风眼。他在港里设埋伏，只盯港里的水位。The inspector does not sail out to hunt storm eyes. He sets a trap in the harbour and watches only the water level.

先兑一桶水。别的港口记账带正负号。比如按除以 4 的余数分家的那个港口，余 1 的素数记加一，余 3 的记减一。主港不分家，每个素数都记加一。探长把两本账兑进一只桶，主港按整份，另一家只兑七分之一。这样每一笔最少也剩七分之六，总是正的。First he mixes a tank. The other harbours keep signed books. The harbour that sorts by remainder mod 4, for instance, writes +1 for a prime leaving remainder 1 and −1 for one leaving remainder 3. The main harbour sorts nothing and writes +1 for every prime. The inspector pours both books into one tank, the main harbour at full strength and the other at one seventh. Every entry then keeps at least six sevenths, always positive.

为什么非要全是正数？打个比方，两个人抬一张桌子，一个往上抬、一个往下压，桌子看着不动，你就看不出有人在使劲。账里全是正数，就没有人往下压。东边要是藏着风眼，它掀起的浪没法被别的浪抵消，迟早会把水位顶上去。这是 Landau 在 1905 年证明过的一条老道理。Why must everything be positive? Picture two people holding a table, one lifting and one pressing down: the table stays still and you cannot tell anyone is pushing. With every entry positive, nobody presses down. An eye hidden in the east cannot have its swell cancelled by others, and sooner or later it pushes the level up. This is an old principle Landau proved in 1905.

于是探长立了一条警戒线，高度是“数到的那个数开平方，再高一点点”。东边要是有风眼，它的浪越来越高，走到远处，水面每隔一段就会冲过这条线。反过来，只要在远处总能找到一整段水面，从头到尾都没冲过线，就说明东边没有风眼。这“一点点”可以随便取多小，取得越小，排除的范围就越贴近中线；小到没有尽头，东边就一个风眼也藏不下。So the inspector draws a warning line at the square root of the count, plus a little. If there is an eye in the east, its swell keeps growing, and far out the water crosses the line again and again. Turned around: if far out you can always find a whole stretch of water that never crosses the line, there is no eye in the east. The “little” can be as small as you like; the smaller it is, the closer to the middle line the ruled-out region reaches, and with no limit to how small, no eye can hide in the east at all.

水位往下落多深，不用管，这条线只管上面。剩下的全部功夫，都花在证明这条线守得住。How low the water falls does not matter; the line only watches the top. Everything that follows goes into proving the line holds.

现实里 · 给懂行的读者In reality · for readers who know the field桶里的水叫 Gm，是一族狄利克雷特征的平滑切比雪夫和，兑的比例是 1/(3o+1)，o 是这一族的个数。档案自己证明的窗口判据是：若任意远处都有区间 [Y, Y1.5]（例如从一万到一百万），在整段上 Gm(y) ≤ yθ，则实部大于 θ 处没有零点。最后守住的警戒线是 y^(1/2+9ε)，ε 可以任意小。这是单边上界，不是水位的精确公式。The tank is Gm, smoothed Chebyshev sums over a family of Dirichlet characters mixed in the ratio 1/(3o+1), where o is the size of the family. The archive's window criterion: if arbitrarily far out there are intervals [Y, Y1.5] (say from ten thousand to a million) on which Gm(y) ≤ yθ throughout, there is no zero with real part above θ. The line finally held is y^(1/2+9ε) with ε as small as you like. It is a one-sided bound, not a formula for the level.
第 4 章Chapter 4

内湖旧案The inland-lake case

怎么证明水面不过线？探长翻出一桩旧案。内湖里的问题是素数问题的迷你版，同样是数东西，同样要把误差压到根号，而在那里，这件事几十年前就办成了。内湖是一个只有有限个数的小世界，像钟面算术。最简单的只有 0 到 6 这七个数，5 加 4 得 9，超过 6 就减去 7，得 2。在这样的湖里画一条弯曲的湖岸，问上面有几个点。答案大约就是湖里数的个数，难的是把误差压到这个个数开平方的几倍以内。How to prove the water stays under the line? The inspector pulls out an old case. The lake's problem is a miniature of the prime problem, again counting things and again needing the error down to a square root, and there it was solved decades ago. The inland lake is a small world with finitely many numbers, like clock arithmetic. The simplest has just the seven numbers 0 to 6: 5 plus 4 is 9, and past 6 you subtract 7, giving 2. Draw a curved shore in such a lake and ask how many points it has. The answer is roughly the number of numbers in the lake; the hard part is holding the error to a few times its square root.

1969 年 Stepanov 用到了中学学过的一件事。一个 36 次的算式（也就是 x 的最高次方是 36 的多项式，比如 x³ − 2x + 1 是 3 次的）最多有 36 个根，重根按重数算，比如 (x−2)³ 在 2 这里算三个根。他专门设计了一个算式，让湖岸上的点几乎全是它的根，而且每个都是 m 重根。根的总数不能超过 36，所以点数乘 m 不超过 36。m 取 3，最多 12 个点；m 取 4，最多 9 个点。叠得越厚，点数就被压得越紧。In 1969 Stepanov used something from school. A polynomial of degree 36 has at most 36 roots, counting repeated roots with multiplicity: (x−2)³ counts three times at 2. He designed a polynomial whose roots include almost every point of the shore, each as a root of multiplicity m. The roots cannot number more than 36, so the number of points times m is at most 36. With m = 3 there are at most 12 points; with m = 4, at most 9. The thicker the stacking, the tighter the count.

这个算式设计得很巧，不用先知道点在哪里。难处在于层数叠得越厚，算式的次数也得跟着变大；Stepanov 的本事是把这笔账算得很紧，结果点数只比正常值多出根号那么一点。1973 年 Bombieri 把这个办法理得更顺。The polynomial is cleverly designed so that nobody needs to know where the points are. The catch is that thicker stacking forces a higher degree; Stepanov's skill was balancing the two so tightly that the count exceeds the expected number by only about a square root. Bombieri streamlined the method in 1973.

探长想把这个办法搬到大海上，第一步就卡住了。内湖里，让一个点一口气叠好几层，靠的是求导数，整数世界没有这件工具。作者的说法是，在整数上找重数，相当于给整数求导。叠层得另找门路。The inspector tries to take this out to sea and is stuck at once. On the lake, stacking several layers on one point is done with derivatives, and the world of whole numbers has no such tool. In the author's words, finding multiplicity over the integers corresponds to something like differentiating whole numbers. The stacking has to come from somewhere else.

现实里 · 给懂行的读者In reality · for readers who know the field封条卷是一个辅助函数，36 是它的次数，“叠 m 层”是它在这一点消失到 m 阶；少数点可以例外，另外记账。“不用知道点在哪里”靠的是：在七个数的湖里，每个数的 7 次方都等于它自己（3 的 7 次方是 2187，除以 7 余 3），这是费马小定理，也是 Frobenius 映射的雏形。Alpöge 说这份证明是 Stepanov 方法的后代。The roll of seals is an auxiliary function, 36 its degree, and “m layers” means vanishing to order m at the point; a few points may be exceptions, counted separately. Not needing to know where the points are rests on this: in the seven-number lake every number equals its own seventh power (3 to the 7th is 2187, remainder 3 on division by 7). That is Fermat's little theorem, the seed of the Frobenius map. Alpöge calls the proof a descendant of Stepanov's.
第 5 章Chapter 5

一串船闸A chain of locks

先看整数里什么像叠层。7000 末尾有三个零，它被 10 整除了三次。一个数只要不是 0，末尾又有三个零，它至少是 1000。零越多，数被逼得越大。这和内湖的规矩是一回事：一个算式不为零，又在某点有三重根，它至少是 3 次。整数末尾的零对应算式的重根，整数的位数对应算式的次数。所以在整数世界，“能被 p 连除几次”可以顶替“叠了几层”。First, what counts as stacking among whole numbers? 7000 ends in three zeros: it is divisible by 10 three times. A number that is not 0 and ends in three zeros is at least 1000. More zeros force it to be bigger. This is the lake's rule in another form: a non-zero polynomial with a triple root somewhere has degree at least 3. Trailing zeros of a number match repeated roots of a polynomial, and the number of digits matches the degree. So among whole numbers, “how many times p divides it” can stand in for “how many layers are stacked”.

难处是，没有现成的办法逼着一个数被 p 连除很多次。两人的办法在运河上。港口后面有条运河，河上有一种老船闸，数学家叫它 theta 提升，用了很多年。货每过一道闸，货单上写的几个关键的数，就能被 p 多除几次。两人把船闸一道接一道串起来，过完整条运河，这些数要么干脆是 0，要么能被 p 连除很多次。连除的次数，就顶替了内湖里的层数。The difficulty is that nothing forces a number to be divisible by p many times. The two found their way on the canal. Behind the harbour runs a canal with an old kind of lock, which mathematicians call a theta lift and have used for many years. Each time a cargo passes a lock, a few key numbers on its papers become divisible by p a few more times. The two chained the locks one after another; after the whole canal, those numbers are either 0 or divisible by p many times over. That count stands in for the layers on the lake.

要串多少道？搭档报了个数，√X 道。拿数到一百万来算，开平方是一千。误差里有一项是“两百万除以闸数”。串 100 道，这一项是 2 万，远远超过一千；串 1000 道，降到 2000，和一千同一个量级。可账上还有一项随闸数往上涨，串得太多它又冒出来。两头一夹，只剩一千道上下这条窄缝。内湖里层数也取在根号附近，这里一道闸顶一层，闸数取根号就顺理成章。How many locks? The partner named a number: √X. Count to a million, whose square root is a thousand. One term of the error is two million divided by the number of locks. With 100 locks it is 20,000, far above a thousand; with 1000 locks it falls to 2000, the same size as a thousand. But another term grows with the number of locks, so too many locks bring it back. Squeezed from both sides, only a narrow gap around a thousand locks is left. On the lake the layers are also taken near the square root, and here each lock stands for a layer, so a square-root number of locks is the natural choice.

“Claude 建议的 theta 提升链长度是 √X。（可除此之外，还能是什么呢？）”“Claude's suggested length of chain of theta lifts … was √X. (And yet, what else could it be?)”
Alpöge 的原话。他说单这一句，就足以让同行从椅子上摔下来。Alpöge's words. He says this one line will suffice to make fellow mathematicians fall out of their chairs.
现实里 · 给懂行的读者In reality · for readers who know the field船闸是全定酉群之间的 theta 提升，链是 U(X0)→U(X1)→…→U(Xm)；“被 p 多除几次”是某些周期的 p 进整除，每层是扣除分母损失后的净收益，各层不同，档案没有“每层固定加一”。2x/m 是档案误差估计里四项中的第一项，它要求 m ≥ x^(1/2−ε)，随闸数增长的那一项要求 m ≤ x^(1/2+ε/2)。theta 提升是早就有的工具，这次的新意是把它串成链，用来代替重数。The locks are theta lifts between definite unitary groups, the chain is U(X0)→U(X1)→…→U(Xm); “divisible by p a few more times” is p-adic divisibility of certain periods, each layer contributing a net gain after a denominator loss, different from layer to layer, with no uniform “plus one”. 2x/m is the first of four terms in the archive's error estimate and needs m ≥ x^(1/2−ε); the term that grows with the chain needs m ≤ x^(1/2+ε/2). Theta lifts are an established tool; what is new is chaining them to stand in for multiplicity.
第 6 章Chapter 6

卡在三分之二Stuck at two-thirds

账细算下来，两人撞上了一堵墙。每串一道闸，账上都要付一笔代价，就是上一章那个随闸数往上涨的项。按最初算出的代价，警戒线只能压到“数到的数的三分之二次方”。数到一百万，要守的线是一千，三分之二次方是一万，高了十倍；数到一万亿，一边是一百万，一边是一亿，高了一百倍。越往远差得越多，这样的线拦不住风眼。When the books were worked out, the two hit a wall. Every lock in the chain costs something: the term from the last chapter that grows with the number of locks. With that cost as first computed, the warning line could only be pushed down to the count raised to the power two-thirds. Counting to a million, the line to hold is a thousand and the two-thirds power is ten thousand, ten times too high; counting to a trillion it is a million against a hundred million, a hundred times too high. The further out, the worse; a line like that cannot stop the eyes.

翻过去靠两招。第一招叫“除法”，道理像换记账单位。一个整数只要不是 0，最小也是 1。如果还知道它是 1000 的倍数，那它不是 0 就至少是 1000。过完闸，货单上的数都能被 p 连除很多次。比方它们个个是 1000 的倍数，那就改用“千”做单位，24000 记成 24，7000 记成 7。账面上照样全是整数，“不是 0 就至少是 1”这条规矩照样管用，力气却大了一千倍。这一招的用处是让账算得更紧，同一本账换成更大的单位，记出来的数更小，误差的上限也就跟着压下来。作者的时间线还提到一条叫 Bost 斜率不等式的老规矩，它是这条道理的加强版，一次管一大批数。Two moves got them over. The first is called “division”, and it works like changing the unit of account. A whole number that is not 0 is at least 1. If it is also known to be a multiple of 1000, then unless it is 0 it is at least 1000. After the locks, the numbers on the papers are divisible by p many times. Suppose each is a multiple of 1000: switch to thousands as the unit, so 24,000 is written 24 and 7,000 is written 7. The books still hold whole numbers, and the rule “not 0 means at least 1” still applies, now with a thousand times the force. The point is to make the books tighter: the same account in a larger unit records smaller numbers, and the bound on the error comes down with them. The author's timeline also mentions an old rule called the Bost slope inequality, a stronger form of the same principle that handles a whole batch of numbers at once.

第二招作者叫“最大公约数”，意思是把几本账并成一本，多平均一次。The second move the author calls a “gcd”: read several ledgers together as one, and average once more.

作者附的时间线（他说是 Claude 仔细检索后整理的）从 10 月 6 日的“Stepanov 行列式”开始，船闸链、⅔ 那堵墙和这两招都记在 11 月 15 日。那天是周日。Alpöge 写道，一切是在一个周日深夜成的。五天后，证明公布。The author's timeline, which he says Claude compiled after a thorough search, begins on 6 October with “Stepanov determinant”. The chain of locks, the wall at ⅔ and both moves are dated 15 November, a Sunday. Alpöge writes that it all happened for him late on a Sunday night. Five days later the proof was public.

现实里 · 给懂行的读者In reality · for readers who know the field“除法”是用分式理想放大一个格，配对仍为整数，整除换成格的斜率收益；“最大公约数”是跨 Hecke 特征粘合整结构。⅔ 和这两招出自作者时间线第 14、15 条，Bost 斜率不等式见第 13 条。按配套导读的读法，斜率收益经高度预算进入误差估计；它们具体落在代码哪里，导读还没追清。“Division” enlarges a lattice by a fractional ideal while keeping the pairings integral, turning divisibility into a gain in slope; the “gcd” glues integral structures across Hecke characters. The ⅔ and both moves come from items 14 and 15 of the author's timeline, the Bost slope inequality from item 13. As the companion guide reads it, the slope gain enters the error estimate through a height budget; where exactly this happens in the code has not been traced yet.
第 7 章Chapter 7

纸里的水印Watermarks in the paper

还有一处漏洞。货单上的数能被 p 整除，不一定是船闸的功劳。有的数天生就带着 p，好比纸出厂就有水印。数学家早就知道这种“天生带 p”的事。要是把水印当成闸上盖的印，账就算错了。One gap remains. If a number on the papers is divisible by p, the locks may not deserve the credit. Some numbers carry p from birth, like paper that leaves the mill with a watermark; mathematicians have long known such cases. Count a watermark as a lock's stamp and the books come out wrong.

探长的办法是挑纸。他只用一族特别挑过的纸，再证明，在他选定的那几段水面上，这种纸上的水印加起来有个上限，和主账比只是零头，记进误差就行。作者说，这一步相当于数论里一个老猜想（Vandiver 猜想）的大幅弱化版。The inspector's answer is to choose the paper. He uses only one specially chosen family of paper and proves that, on the stretches of water he picked, the watermarks on such paper add up to a capped total, small change next to the main account, which can go into the error. The author calls this a significant weakening of an old conjecture in number theory, Vandiver's.

现实里 · 给懂行的读者In reality · for readers who know the field“天生带 p”的经典例子：第 12 个伯努利数是 −691/2730，691 整除它的分子，这对应 691 次分圆域的类数能被 691 整除（Kummer）；Herbrand 和 Ribet 把这类对应精确化。档案里的水印是 Tate–Shafarevich 型群（Sha）的长度；纸是一族选定的分圆域的 CM 子域，挑选条件包括一个辅助素数除以 8 余 3、另一个除以 16 余 7；上限是 exp(x^ε/2)，对族里每个域分别成立，只在选定窗口上成立。Vandiver 猜想最早见于 Kummer 1849 年写给 Kronecker 的信；作者的“弱化版”说法在代码里还没有找到精确对应。A classical case of a factor of p “from birth”: the twelfth Bernoulli number is −691/2730, 691 divides its numerator, and this corresponds to 691 dividing the class number of the 691st cyclotomic field (Kummer); Herbrand and Ribet made such correspondences precise. In the archive the watermarks are lengths of Tate–Shafarevich-type groups (Sha); the paper is a selected family of CM subfields of cyclotomic fields, chosen with conditions such as one auxiliary prime leaving remainder 3 mod 8 and another leaving 7 mod 16; the bound is exp(x^ε/2), for each field of the family separately and only on the chosen windows. Vandiver's conjecture goes back to a letter of Kummer to Kronecker in 1849; no precise counterpart of the author's “weakening” has been found in the code yet.
第 8 章Chapter 8

十七万册卷宗A hundred and seventy-five thousand files

前面几章是破案的主线。要让每一步都站得住，底下压着的老案卷也得一册一册重新核过，那是一百多年来数论最深的一批成果，以前大多只写在论文里。证明里引用前人定理的地方有 185 处，每一处档案都在 Lean 里自己证了一遍，没有一处是“先信着再说”。The chapters so far are the main line of the case. For every step to hold, the old files underneath had to be re-examined one by one as well: some of the deepest results of more than a century of number theory, mostly found only in papers until now. The proof cites earlier theorems at 185 places, and at every one the archive proves the result in Lean itself; nothing is taken on trust.

卷宗一共 175,187 册，约 1740 万行。一个人一秒读一行，不吃不睡，要读 201 天。其中 99,187 册摆在同一个书架上，标签是“阿贝尔簇理论”。整个顶层搭在三块基石上，分别是船闸那一套的数据、第 7 章的水印上限，和一条 1955 年的经典定理。从三块基石推到黎曼猜想，用到其中 17,276 册。The archive has 175,187 files, about 17.4 million lines. Reading one line a second, without food or sleep, would take 201 days. Of these, 99,187 sit on one shelf labelled “abelian variety theory”. The top of the argument rests on three cornerstones: the data from the locks, the watermark bound of Chapter 7, and a classical theorem from 1955. Getting from those three to the Riemann hypothesis uses 17,276 of the files.

探长也试过把证明展开成给人读的书，一印就是 25 万多页，搭档管它叫《GRH 之书》。他翻开定理 1，第一句是：因为实数可以比大小，若 σ ≠ ½，则 σ > ½ 或 σ < ½。他合上书，决定只公布 Lean 代码。The inspector also tried expanding the proof into a book for people. It came to more than 250,000 pages, and the partner called it the GRH “book”. He opened Theorem 1. It began by noting that because the reals are totally ordered, if σ ≠ ½ then σ > ½ or σ < ½. He closed it and decided to publish only the Lean.

Green 1955 年关于有限一般线性群特征标的工作）。顶层那 17,276 个文件里，有 4,057 个也在阿贝尔簇目录里；三块基石本身的证明在其余文件中。《GRH 之书》和定理 1 出自 Alpöge 的说明。The old files include the local Langlands correspondence, Galois representations, p-adic Hodge theory, arithmetic geometry and class field theory. 185 counts entries, not distinct theorems; some are placeholders or forwarders. Of the 17,276 top-level files, 4,057 also sit in the abelian variety directory; the proofs of the cornerstones are in the other files. The GRH “book” and its Theorem 1 come from Alpöge's remark.

验卷Checking the file

The MIT mathematician Andrew Sutherland ran several independently written checkers over the archive. By late on 20 November every finished check had passed, and the whole proof uses only the three most basic axioms; two of the slowest checks were still running.

后记Afterword

为什么先倒下的是它Why this one fell first

黎曼猜想对人来说难得惊人，可它有一样哲学没有的东西：题目能写成一行精确的定义，答案对不对能交给机器去判。这有两层用处。交卷以后，几台独立的检查器一天之内就能确认推理没错；做题的时候，每一步也有对错可查，AI 可以在人跟不上的地方一路试下去。一部“AI 写的黑格尔”没有这样的裁判，它有没有新东西、值不值得读，只能靠人慢慢读、慢慢争。The Riemann hypothesis is staggeringly hard for people, but it has something philosophy lacks: the question fits in one exact definition, and whether an answer is right can be left to machines. That helps twice. After the answer is handed in, independent checkers can confirm the reasoning within a day; while the problem is being worked, every step can be checked too, so an AI can keep going where people cannot follow. An “AI Hegel” would have no such referee: whether it says anything new, and whether it is worth reading, can only be settled by people reading and arguing, slowly.

所以先被承认的，是有裁判的那类成果。裁判只管“对”，管不了“懂”。这份证明要真正成为人的数学，还得等有人把它讲出来；到了那一关，它和哲学面对的是同一批读者。So the results that come with a referee are the first to be accepted. But a referee rules on “correct”, not on “understood”. For this proof to become human mathematics, someone still has to explain it, and at that stage it faces the same readers as philosophy.
